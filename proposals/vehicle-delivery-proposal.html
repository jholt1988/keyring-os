Purpose of Agreement

This proposal outlines a mutually beneficial arrangement for the use of a vehicle to operate commercial delivery services through platforms such as DoorDash, Spark, and similar services. The purpose of this agreement is to establish clear terms that benefit both parties while maintaining the vehicle's condition and value.

Shared Vision: This arrangement is designed to be a short-term partnership that creates value for both parties. The vehicle user will work diligently to maximize earnings while the owner receives guaranteed compensation and assistance with vehicle-related and household expenses.

Vehicle Information

The following vehicles are subject to this agreement:

  • Nissan Altima: 2015, Red — Primary delivery vehicle (borrowed)
  • Dodge Dakota: 1999, Grey — Secondary vehicle
  • Truck: Available for use on certain platforms as needed

Vehicles will be designated for delivery use as mutually agreed upon by both parties.

Terms and Conditions

1. Vehicle Care and Maintenance

Cleanliness
Vehicle User agrees to maintain the vehicle in clean condition at all times, including regular interior and exterior cleaning.
Fuel
Vehicle User agrees to keep the vehicle full of gas and return it with adequate fuel for the next use.
General Care
Vehicle User agrees to operate the vehicle responsibly, follow all traffic laws, and report any mechanical issues promptly.

2. Compensation Structure

Payment Summary

Base Vehicle Rent $250.00 per month
Revenue Share (20% of earnings) 20% of monthly delivery income
Estimated Weekly Earnings $700 - $1,300 per week
Estimated Monthly (20% share) $560 - $1,040 per month
Total Monthly Payment $250 + 20% of earnings

3. Additional Vehicle Expenses

Dodge - Registration/Tags
Vehicle User agrees to pay for annual registration and tags for the Dodge Dakota.
Nissan - Tire Replacement
Vehicle User agrees to replace tires on the Nissan Altima as needed.
Truck - Tire Replacement (When Used)
Vehicle User agrees to replace tires on the truck when used for certain platforms.

4. Household and Rental Assistance

Vehicle User agrees to provide assistance with the following:

  • Help with rental property management tasks as needed
  • Assistance with household chores and maintenance
  • General support around the residence as mutually agreed

Work Schedule and Time

The specific hours and days of operation will be determined through discussion between both parties. The following items will be addressed during scheduling discussions:

  • Days and hours available for delivery work
  • Scheduling around other commitments and obligations
  • Flexibility for unexpected schedule changes
  • Vehicle availability for personal use by the owner

Both parties agree to communicate openly about scheduling needs and to provide reasonable notice for any changes to the schedule.

Duration and Exit Strategy

This agreement is understood to be a temporary arrangement with the following terms:

  • Duration: This agreement will remain in effect until Vehicle User is able to purchase their own vehicle.
  • Savings Goal: Vehicle User commits to actively saving money with the goal of purchasing a personal vehicle as soon as financially feasible.
  • Flexibility: Either party may request modifications to this agreement with reasonable notice.
  • End of Agreement: Upon Vehicle User's purchase of a personal vehicle, this agreement may transition to a different arrangement or conclude.

Income Potential: Based on current market conditions and platform availability, Vehicle User can reasonably expect to earn between $700 and $1,300 per week through DoorDash, Spark, and other delivery services. This translates to approximately $560 - $1,040 monthly from the 20% revenue share.

Mutual Benefit: This arrangement allows Vehicle User to earn income and build savings while providing the Owner with additional income, household assistance, and help with vehicle expenses. Both parties benefit from this cooperation.
